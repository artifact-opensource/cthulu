# K1 — The Tentacle

> A single sensory pod. One timeframe. Eight indicators. No reasoning — only reading.

---

## What Is a Tentacle

K1 through K8 are Cthulu's sensory organs. Each tentacle is locked to a single timeframe (M1, M5, M15, M30, H1, H4, D1, W1) and runs eight technical indicators against incoming price data. A tentacle does not decide. It observes, grades the quality of what it sees, and reports upstream to K9.

The design is deliberately simple. Speed over intelligence. A tentacle scans in ~4.7ms. Thirty-two tentacles (4 symbols × 8 timeframes) complete in under 40ms. The intelligence lives in K9 — the tentacles exist to feed it clean, graded, regime-tagged signals.

## Indicators

Each tentacle computes eight indicators per scan:

### 1. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- **Period:** 14
- **Type:** Momentum oscillator
- **Signal logic:** RSI > 70 → SELL (overbought). RSI < 30 → BUY (oversold). Between → NEUTRAL.
- **Behavior:** Directional only ~30% of the time. Most bars are neutral. This is correct — RSI fires at extremes, not continuously.

### 2. MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- **Periods:** Fast 12, slow 26, signal 9
- **Type:** Trend following
- **Signal logic:** Histogram > 0 AND MACD > signal → BUY. Histogram < 0 AND MACD < signal → SELL. Crossover events generate STRONG signals.
- **Behavior:** Lagging but reliable for trend confirmation. Cross detection adds timing.

### 3. ATR (Average True Range)
- **Period:** 14
- **Type:** Volatility measure
- **Signal logic:** Always NEUTRAL. ATR does not generate directional signals.
- **Purpose:** Consumed by K9 for position sizing, SL/TP calculation, and volatility regime detection. Critical infrastructure, not a signal source.

### 4. ADX (Average Directional Index)
- **Period:** 14
- **Type:** Trend strength
- **Signal logic:** ADX > 25 indicates trending market. Direction determined by +DI vs -DI comparison. ADX < 25 → NEUTRAL (ranging market).
- **Behavior:** The gatekeeper — tells you whether a trend exists before you trade it.

### 5. Bollinger Bands
- **Period:** 20, Standard deviations: 2
- **Type:** Mean reversion / volatility envelope
- **Signal logic:** %B > 0.95 → SELL (price at upper band). %B < 0.05 → BUY (price at lower band). Between → NEUTRAL.
- **Output:** Also provides upper, lower, middle band values and %B for K9's level extraction.

### 6. Stochastic Oscillator
- **Periods:** K=14, D=3
- **Type:** Momentum
- **Signal logic:** %K > 80 → SELL (overbought). %K < 20 → BUY (oversold).
- **Behavior:** Similar cadence to RSI. Fires at extremes. Neutral most of the time.

### 7. EMA Cross (Exponential Moving Average)
- **Periods:** Fast 9, slow 21
- **Type:** Trend confirmation
- **Signal logic:** Fast > slow → BUY. Fast < slow → SELL. Crossover event → STRONG signal.
- **Behavior:** The most reliable directional indicator. Almost always has an opinion. Crossover events are high-value signals.

### 8. Supertrend
- **Period:** 10, Multiplier: 3
- **Type:** Trend filter
- **Signal logic:** Price > upper band → BUY. Price < lower band → SELL.
- **Behavior:** Lagging but stable. Rarely flips in noise. Good for trend confirmation on higher timeframes.

## Signal Aggregation

Indicators vote. Each directional indicator (excluding ATR, which is always neutral) contributes a weighted signal:

```
Directional count = number of indicators with BUY or SELL opinion
Agreement ratio = max(bullish_count, bearish_count) / directional_count
Direction = majority vote (BUY or SELL)
Strength = STRONG (agreement > 0.8) or normal
```

**Classification scale:**
- `STRONG_BUY` (+2) — high agreement, bullish
- `BUY` (+1)
- `NEUTRAL` (0) — no clear consensus
- `SELL` (-1)
- `STRONG_SELL` (-2) — high agreement, bearish

**Important:** At any given moment, only 3-5 of the 7 directional indicators will have an opinion. RSI, Bollinger, and Stochastic are neutral 60-70% of the time by design. A tentacle grade accounts for this — grading on quality of directional agreement, not on how many indicators fired.

## Grading

Each tentacle scan produces a grade from A+ to F:

```
score = agreement_confidence × 0.75 + breadth_bonus × 0.25

A+ > 0.85    (exceptional convergence)
A  > 0.70    (strong convergence)
B  > 0.55    (moderate)
C  > 0.40    (weak)
D  > 0.25    (poor)
F  ≤ 0.25    (no usable signal)
```

The grade tells K9 how much to trust this tentacle's opinion. An A+ from H4 carries significant weight. An F from M1 is noise.

## Regime Detection

Each tentacle classifies the current market regime using two inputs:

1. **Linear regression slope** — direction and strength of recent price movement
2. **ATR-to-price ratio** — normalized volatility measure

**Regimes:**
- `trending_up` — strong positive slope, moderate volatility
- `trending_down` — strong negative slope, moderate volatility
- `volatile` — high ATR relative to price, any slope
- `ranging` — low slope, moderate volatility

Regime tags flow upstream to K9, where the confluence engine looks for regime consensus across timeframes. When M5, M15, M30, and H1 all agree the market is trending up — that's a real trend, not noise.

## Timeframe Weights

Not all tentacles are equal. K9 weights higher timeframes more heavily:

| Tentacle | Timeframe | Weight |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| K1 | M1 | 0.5 |
| K2 | M5 | 0.7 |
| K3 | M15 | 1.0 |
| K4 | M30 | 1.2 |
| K5 | H1 | 1.5 |
| K6 | H4 | 2.0 |
| K7 | D1 | 2.5 |
| K8 | W1 | 3.0 |

Weekly signals carry 6× the weight of M1 signals. Higher timeframes represent more data, more market participants, and less noise.

## What a Tentacle Does NOT Do

- **Does not decide to trade.** That's K9's job.
- **Does not know about other timeframes.** Each tentacle is isolated.
- **Does not track history.** Signal memory lives in K9's confluence engine.
- **Does not manage positions.** Risk management lives in K9.
- **Does not reason.** It reads. It feels. It reports.

The intelligence is emergent — it arises from K9's synthesis of all eight tentacles, not from any individual one.
