# K9 — The Brain

> The ninth kube. Eight tentacles sense. One brain thinks. Together: an organism.

---

## What Is K9

K9 is the cognition layer. It receives graded, regime-tagged signals from all eight tentacles and synthesizes them into trade decisions through four subsystems:

1. **Hemisphere 1 (Confluence Engine)** — Where do timeframes agree?
2. **Hemisphere 2 (Prediction Engine)** — Where is price going?
3. **Rationale Engine** — Why this trade? How? Where?
4. **Risk Manager** — Can we afford it?

A decision requires all four to participate. Confluence says the signal is real. Prediction says where it's going. Rationale explains why and how to enter. Risk says whether the account can survive it. If any component dissents, the decision is HOLD.

This is alive architecture. Not modular-replaceable. The entire system needs every component or it faces death.

## Hemisphere 1 — Confluence Engine

**Purpose:** Find where independent timeframes arrive at the same truth independently.

Not averaging. Not majority vote. Convergence. When M5, M15, M30, and H1 independently conclude "bullish" — using different indicator configurations, different data windows, different noise profiles — that agreement is a real signal.

### 10-Phase Analysis Pipeline

**Phase 1 — Direction Classification**
Count bullish, bearish, and neutral tentacles across all 8 timeframes.

**Phase 2 — Weighted Direction**
Apply timeframe weights (M1: 0.5 through W1: 3.0). A bullish W1 contributes 6× more than a bullish M1.

**Phase 3 — Confluence Strength**
Classify the agreement level:
- UNANIMOUS — 7-8 tentacles agree
- DOMINANT — 5-6 agree
- MAJORITY — 4 agree
- SPLIT — 3 agree
- CONTRADICTORY — no clear majority

**Phase 4 — Timeframe Harmony**
Detect adjacent timeframe agreement chains. M5→M15→M30→H1 agreeing is a stronger signal than M1+H4+W1 agreeing, because adjacent timeframes should naturally diverge. When they don't — when the signal persists across consecutive scales — it's structural, not noise.

Harmony chain length and location matter. A chain in the middle timeframes (M15→M30→H1→H4) is more actionable than one at the extremes.

**Phase 5 — Quality Metrics**
Average grade score across tentacles. Count of A+ signals. Quality measures how confident each individual tentacle is in its own reading.

**Phase 6 — Regime Consensus**
Mode of regime detections across timeframes, weighted by timeframe importance. When 6 of 8 tentacles see "trending_up," the regime is trending. Mixed regimes increase uncertainty.

**Phase 7 — 4-Pillar Confluence Score (max 10.0)**

| Pillar | Max | What It Measures |
|--------|-----|------------------|
| Agreement | 3.5 | Do timeframes converge? |
| Quality | 2.5 | Are individual signals confident? |
| Direction | 2.5 | Is the weighted direction strong? |
| Harmony | 1.5 | Do adjacent TFs form chains? |

Each pillar is independently meaningful. A score of 7.5+ is A+ grade. 5.5+ is A grade. Below 4.0, the signal isn't worth trading.

**Phase 8 — PUP Uncertainty Quantification**
Propagated Uncertainty Principle. Uncertainty starts at 1.0 (maximum) and is reduced multiplicatively by evidence:

```
U = 1.0 × (1 - agreement × 0.4)
        × (1 - quality × 0.3)
        × (1 - direction × 0.2)
        × (1 - harmony × 0.1)

If CONTRADICTORY: U = min(U × 1.3, 1.0)
```

Each evidence layer independently shrinks the space of uncertainty. Contradiction inflates it. The system quantifies what it doesn't know.

**Phase 9 — Trade Decision**
- A+/A grade → ACT
- B + strong direction → CAUTIOUS (may act with reduced size)
- C or below → HOLD
- Volatile regime raises the entry threshold

**Phase 10 — Entry Parameters**
SL/TP computed from ATR values, preferring H4 > H1 > D1 for stability (higher TF ATR is less noisy).

### Signal Memory

Stores the last 50-100 confluence results per symbol. Tracks:
- Direction trend — strengthening, weakening, or stable
- Score trend — improving, degrading, or stable
- Grade transitions — was it A yesterday and C today?
- Action stability — how often the decision changes

This gives K9 a sense of momentum-of-momentum. Not just "is it bullish?" but "is the bullishness growing?"

## Hemisphere 2 — Prediction Engine

**Purpose:** Where is price going? How confident are we?

The prediction hemisphere is an ensemble architecture. Multiple models contribute forecasts, weighted by their proven track record. As models train and demonstrate edge, they earn more influence.

### Ensemble Members

| Model | Status | Method | Weight |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Naive** | Active | ATR-scaled directional forecast, sigmoid probabilities | 1.0 |
| **HMM** | Stub | 4-state Hidden Markov Model (bull/bear/ranging/volatile) | 0.0 |
| **GLADIUS** | Stub | 6.9M parameter transformer kernel (SLA², Time2Vec, MoE, Warm Memory) | 0.0 |

The naive baseline always contributes — it establishes the floor. As HMM and GLADIUS train and prove edge on real data, their weights increase and the ensemble's foresight improves.

### Output Per Symbol

- Price predictions at 1H, 4H, 1D horizons
- 95% confidence intervals (ATR-based)
- Direction probabilities: P(up), P(down), P(flat) — softmax output
- Regime prediction with confidence
- Method label and model confidence

### The GLADIUS Integration Path

H2 is not just a prediction module — it is GLADIUS's training ground for market intuition:

```
Tentacles SENSE → H2 LEARNS → Feeds GLADIUS → GLADIUS REASONS → Controls Cthulu EXECUTION
```

1. Tentacles stream market data
2. H2 trains its predictors (HMM, then GLADIUS seed) on that data
3. Trained model checkpoints get implanted into the full GLADIUS kernel
4. GLADIUS, now with market intuition, reverse-connects to Cthulu
5. GLADIUS generates strategies — not hardcoded rules, but learned reasoning

The difference between a soldier following orders and a general planning the campaign.

## Rationale Engine

**Purpose:** Every decision K9 makes — trade or hold — must have a structured thesis explaining WHY and HOW.

### 9-Phase Rationale Construction

**Phase 1 — Market Structure Identification**

| Structure | Meaning | Entry Approach |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| TRENDING_WITH | Riding the established trend | Aggressive — momentum is on your side |
| TRENDING_AGAINST | Counter-trend | Requires extra conviction, reduced size |
| PULLBACK | Buying the dip / selling the rally | Wait for price to come to you |
| RANGE_BOUNCE | Bouncing off support/resistance | Enter at range boundaries |
| BREAKOUT | Breaking out of a structure | Enter on confirmation |
| EXHAUSTION | Move losing momentum | Dangerous — avoid or limit only |

Determined by: higher-TF vs lower-TF trend comparison, momentum divergence detection, Bollinger band position.

**Phase 2 — Price Level Extraction**
Pulls significant price levels from every indicator across every timeframe:
- Bollinger upper/lower/middle → resistance/support/pivot
- EMA fast/slow → dynamic support/resistance
- Supertrend value → trend-based support/resistance

**Phase 3 — Level Clustering**
Levels within 0.2% of each other across different timeframes cluster together. Multi-TF convergence on the same price level = stronger structural significance.

```
cluster_strength = unique_TF_sources / 4  (capped at 1.0)
```

Four or more timeframes agreeing on the same price level = maximum strength.

**Phase 4 — Order Type Selection**

| Structure | Price Position | Order Type |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| TRENDING_WITH | Good entry zone | MARKET |
| TRENDING_WITH | Overextended | LIMIT (wait for pullback) |
| PULLBACK | In trend | LIMIT (buy the dip) |
| BREAKOUT | Forming | STOP (enter on confirmation) |
| RANGE_BOUNCE | At boundary | LIMIT |
| EXHAUSTION | Any | LIMIT only, extra concerns flagged |

**Phase 5 — Stop Reasoning**
Every SL and TP has a reason, not just a number.

- **SL placement:** Structure-first (behind nearest support/resistance with ATR buffer). ATR-fallback (1.5× ATR from entry) when no clear structure exists.
- **TP placement:** Level-first (next resistance/support giving ≥ 1.5:1 R:R). R:R-fallback (2:1 from entry).
- **Reason string examples:** "Below support at 2140 (BB lower H4)" or "1.5× ATR from entry — no clear structure."

**Phase 6 — Evidence Chain**
Catalogues every supporting data point:
- Confluence strength and grade
- Harmony chain description
- A+ signal locations
- Regime consensus
- Direction strength
- Trend-of-trend from signal memory
- Individual TF breakdowns for strong signals

**Phase 7 — Concern Identification**
What could go wrong:
- Low confidence or high PUP uncertainty
- Counter-trend trading
- Volatile regime
- Weak harmony
- Contradictory signals across timeframes
- Weakening confluence trend
- Too many D/F grades feeding the confluence

**Phase 8 — Invalidation Definition**
Exact conditions that would kill the thesis — a price level and a reason. "If price breaks below 2120 (H4 support), the trend structure is broken."

**Phase 9 — Thesis Construction**
A one-line dynamic summary of WHY the trade makes sense. For HOLDs, explains what's missing. For trades, describes the structural basis.

### Timing Confidence
Separate from confluence confidence. Measures how good the entry is RIGHT NOW:
- Market order in a trending-with structure: high (0.7 × 1.2)
- Limit order waiting for price: moderate (0.4)
- Counter-trend: reduced (× 0.6)
- Overall confidence = 60% confluence + 40% timing

## Risk Manager

**Purpose:** The guardrail between analysis and execution. Every trade passes through here.

### Position Sizing — Half-Kelly Criterion

```
kelly_fraction = confidence × 0.5
adjusted_risk = max_risk_per_trade × kelly_fraction
lot_size = (equity × adjusted_risk) / sl_distance
```

Kelly criterion says bet proportional to your edge. Half-Kelly reduces variance at the cost of slower growth — the correct tradeoff for limited capital. Over-sizing kills accounts. Under-sizing wastes edge.

### Drawdown Circuit Breakers

| Level | Trigger | Effect |
|-------|---------|--------|
| NORMAL | Default | Full risk allocation |
| CAUTIOUS | Drawdown ≥ 3% | Risk halved |
| AGGRESSIVE | Reserved | Reserved |
| LOCKDOWN | Drawdown ≥ 8% | No new trades until daily reset |

### Guardrails

- Maximum 4 simultaneous positions
- Maximum 1 position per symbol
- 5% daily loss limit
- **Mandatory SL** — no stop-loss = no trade, no exceptions
- Stop checking on every scan cycle (SL/TP evaluated against current price)
- Daily reset releases lockdown if equity recovers

### Position Lifecycle

Open → update unrealized P&L each scan → check stops → close with reason → record to trade history. Every state transition is logged.

## Decision Synthesis

The core of K9. Where all subsystems converge into a single decision.

### One Scan Cycle

```
1. Check stops on existing positions (before processing new signals)
2. Update equity from current prices
3. For each symbol:
   a. K1-K8 compute signals          (tentacle phase)
   b. H1 finds confluence            (agreement phase)
   c. H2 produces predictions        (foresight phase)
   d. Decision Synthesizer combines  (synthesis phase)
   e. Rationale Engine constructs    (reasoning phase)
   f. Risk Manager gates             (survival phase)
   g. Approved → execution queue     (action phase)
```

### Synthesis Rules

- **H1 (confluence) is the GATE.** Must pass grade threshold or nothing happens.
- **H2 (prediction) is the ADVISOR.** Can boost confidence or veto.
- **Agreement between hemispheres = signal boost** (confidence × 1.2).
- **Disagreement = HOLD.** When in doubt, stay out.
- When prediction is untrained (naive only), lean heavily on confluence.
- As models prove edge, prediction gets more weight in the synthesis.

### Output

`ScanResult` containing:
- Per-symbol confluence (grade, score, direction, regime, harmony chains)
- Per-symbol predictions (direction probabilities, price targets, confidence)
- Per-symbol rationales (thesis, structure, order type, stops, evidence, concerns)
- Execution orders (with full rationale attached)
- Stop events (positions closed by SL/TP)
- Risk summary (level, drawdown, daily P&L)
- Scan timing
